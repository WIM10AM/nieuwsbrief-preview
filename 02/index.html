Newsletter N° 02 / November 2026

October belonged to Amsterdam Dance Event. For Loveland we covered 14 parties in five days, from Paul Kalkbrenner live to Reinier Zonneveld's eight-hour set.

Plus: a brewery that has been brewing since 1758, a fashion night delivered by breakfast, the two films we promised last time and a new client in Ghent.

A RED camera, a clipper and an editor at every party. Recaps and artist clips for Loveland and the artists, while the city was still dancing. From Eastenderz to Paradise, from Mau P to CamelPhat.

From the booth to the brewery

After the dance floor, a different rhythm. Copper kettles in Yorkshire, denim and diamonds in Antwerp. The cocoa fields and coastal roads we couldn't show you last time. And a sports club in Ghent that reopens with a sigh.
