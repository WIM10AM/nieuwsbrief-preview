From festival fields back to set

Festival summer is done, the mud is off the lenses. Corporate season is in full swing. Six projects, from the port of Antwerp to the cocoa fields of Ecuador.

 

Recent work

 

Eight concepts, one visual language. For Antwerp sports club Release we brought eight concepts to the screen, from the studio to the street. Online content, campaign imagery and a facade banner wrapping an entire building. The overview film brings all eight together.

Type: Campaign, online to facade

See more >>
 

Two shoot days. Sixty assets. For Landal we made a glamping campaign for social: one mood film and more than 60 vertical edits, cut-downs and ads for Instagram and Facebook. Built to drive bookings all year round.

Type: Social campaign, photo and video

See more >>
 

Home port. For PSA Antwerp we made an employer branding film aimed at engineers. No actors, just real engineers, on the terminal on Antwerp's Left Bank. The thread running through it: the charging station PSA built in-house for electric straddle carriers. From first meeting to delivery in under three weeks, with a main film and six shorts for social. The first of many, if it's up to us.

Location: Left Bank, Antwerp    Type: Employer branding   

See more >>
 

I love it when a plan comes together. For Biobase and Lucra we went back to the eighties. Our own episode of The A‑Team, with the casting, the costumes and the gold chains done right. Now in the edit.

Type: Parody

Two productions abroad that we can't show yet. With Berlin we travelled to Ecuador, to where Callebaut's cocoa grows, with a stop in Barcelona. The second one stays under wraps for now. Both films go live at the end of October.
